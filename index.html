About

Kourosh Sayehmiri, PhD

Professor of Biostatistics and Head of the Department of Biostatistics at Ilam University of Medical Sciences. For more than 27 years I have designed studies, chosen analysis methods and reported findings for clinical and public-health research — contributing to over 400 published articles and 185+ systematic reviews and meta-analyses, including a Cochrane review.

I hold a PhD and MSc in Biostatistics from Tehran University of Medical Sciences and completed a fellowship in semi-Markov models at the University of Nantes, France. I teach biostatistics, survival analysis, regression, categorical and multivariate data analysis to MSc and PhD students, and have run meta-analysis and statistics workshops at universities in Iran and abroad.

Projects run fully remote: data, questions and drafts move by email, with a call whenever it's quicker to talk.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually once I have seen the question and data. You receive a quote before any work begins.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope: a single analysis is much quicker than a full systematic review. You'll get a timeline together with the quote.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. Your data are used only for your project and are not shared with anyone. Anonymised data are preferred.

Which software will you use?

Whatever suits your team — SPSS, Stata, R, SAS or Python. You receive the syntax so every analysis can be re-run.

Can you help after I submit my paper?

Yes. I support additional analyses and responses to statistical reviewer comments during revision.

Do you run workshops for institutions?

Yes — biostatistics, survival analysis, Stata/R and systematic review & meta-analysis workshops, online or in person.

Have data and a deadline?

Send a short description of your study — I'll reply with the scope, a timeline and a quote.
